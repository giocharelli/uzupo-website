Who we are

A STUDIO BUILT ON OBSESSION.

We find the sounds your story needs.

We are a sound design and foley studio based in Tbilisi, Georgia. What began with one person who fell in love with the craft has grown into an exceptional team, built around that same obsession. We work on feature films, animations, games, and commercials with one consistent standard — we go further than we are asked to go, every time.

Our story

WHERE WE COME FROM.

It started underground. Literally.

A rented basement in Tbilisi. A founder who fell so deep in love with telling stories through sound that the craft became inseparable from who he was. Not a choice he made. A calling that found him. And when a calling finds you, stopping is not an option.

He needed a name. He thought about what he loved — and landed on a kid from a hidden village with an impossible goal and an unbreakable will. Uzumaki. Post-production. uzupo.

How we do it

GREAT SOUND IS FOUND.

Not forced. Not faked. Found.

The world is already full of sound. Our job is to listen deeper than anyone else — to find what belongs in a scene, in a story, in a world — and place it exactly where it needs to be. Sometimes that means stepping outside with a recorder. Sometimes it means reaching for the right piece from a library we trust. Either way, nothing goes in because it's convenient — only because it's right.

That philosophy changes how we approach every project. We don't reach for quick answers. We push further than the brief asks us to go, past where our feet still touch the ground. That is where the real work happens.

Why we do it

THIS IS NOT A JOB.

When you choose sound, you choose a way to live.

The artist's life does not clock out. It does not follow a ladder. It finds inspiration in a footstep, a breath, a door hinge at 3am. It is honest about what it loves and uncompromising in its pursuit.

Most people do not choose this path.

We did. That is what makes uzupo something other than a studio — and the work something other than a service.
