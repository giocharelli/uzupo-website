Field Recording

WE GO INTO THE WORLD TO FIND WHAT BELONGS IN YOUR STORY.

THE CRAFT

Found in the world. Placed in the story.

Great sound is found, not forced. That belief sends us out into the world with recorders, listening for what already exists — the texture of a specific place, the sound of a particular moment, the atmosphere that no library can replicate.

We record sound in the field specifically for the productions we work on. Every recording is chosen, not defaulted. Every texture is real. This is where our core philosophy lives in its purest form — going out and listening until you find exactly what the story needs.

WHEREVER IT TAKES US.

No environment is off-limits.

A specific room tone. The exact wind across a specific ridge. The sound of a city at 4am when nothing else is awake. If a script needs a location's real signature, we go get it — with recorders built for the field, not the studio.

FIELD TO FILM.

Every recording is made for one scene.

We don't pull from a shelf, and we don't recycle a recording from one story into another. Every trip out is made specifically for the scene it's going to serve — and that specificity is exactly what makes it worth the trip.

In the field

If it needs to be performed, we perform it.

Not every location is about standing still and listening. Sometimes the sound you need has to be made — a door, a footstep, an effect built and rebuilt until it's right, out where it actually belongs.

Built for the story.
